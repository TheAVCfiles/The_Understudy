# Evaluation Rubric v0.1

Scores: 0-5.

## Coherence

- 0: no coherent beats or narrative
- 3: beats present but gaps remain
- 5: clear beats with camera continuity

## Innovation

- 0: generic or derivative
- 3: some interesting visual/audio choices
- 5: highly specific theatrical/cinematic language

## Technical

- 0: no actionable tasks or assets
- 3: tasks/assets exist but timeline is incomplete
- 5: clear asset manifest, task assignments, and timeline

The deterministic evaluator should remain stable until Gemini-backed scoring is added behind the adapter boundary.
